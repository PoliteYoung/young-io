# Writing style

## Voice

- Precise, composed, curious, and technically literate.
- Confident about verified work; explicit about uncertainty.
- Prefer strong nouns and verbs over promotional adjectives.
- Explain systems and relationships, not isolated buzzwords.

## Narrative shape

Lead with the problem space and the intersection of disciplines. Follow with selected evidence. Close with the larger system or research question.

Preferred:

> I work where digital-human research meets the engineering and infrastructure required to make it real.

Avoid:

> Passionate tech enthusiast and results-driven expert in cutting-edge AI.

## Channel guidance

- Website: durable identity and selected proof.
- GitHub: implementation evidence and technical contribution.
- LinkedIn: accessible distribution of reviewed updates.
- CV: terse, verifiable, date-precise evidence.
- Speaker bio: audience-relevant narrative, never invented prestige.
