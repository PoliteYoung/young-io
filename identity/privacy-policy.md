# Privacy policy

## Visibility levels

- `public`: approved for public outputs, subject to channel relevance.
- `internal`: may guide private reasoning but must not enter public artifacts.
- `private`: personal data retained only when necessary; never bundled or published.
- `confidential`: restricted information; do not process into generated outputs without explicit, case-specific authorization.

## Public-output gate

A record may be published only when all are true:

1. `visibility: public`
2. the fact is verified or its uncertainty is explicitly appropriate to show
3. its source reference contains no sensitive data
4. the target channel needs it
5. a human has reviewed the generated artifact

## Prohibited content

Never commit credentials, authentication material, government identifiers, private addresses, private contact data, confidential institutional information, unpublished third-party information, or source documents without permission.
